# Changelog

## Unreleased (library)

- `agentsg.cell.clusters`: `cluster_cells(db)` finds every same-protein crystal-form cluster in the PDB cell database: connected components of entries with the same UniProt set (or deposition group without one), a strain price within 7 % over the widened Selling closure, and primitive volumes within 7 %; clusters of more than 10 entries are returned with their medoid, the price from the medoid to every member (chaining shows as members beyond the band) and the joins that built them. `write_clusters` stores the result in DuckDB; `calibrate_key_ratio` measures the key radius needed for the band. `python -m agentsg.cell.pdb_app clusters <db> <out>` runs it from the command line. No HTTP or MCP change.
- `agentsg.cell.strain_closure`: `niggli_from_selling(cell, sg_hm=None)` returns the Niggli cell read off the Selling-reduced superbase, without a second reduction: by Corollary A13 of the manuscript the Niggli vectors are three of the 14 signed partial sums of any obtuse superbase, so the routine tests Niggli's conditions (in the form of the termination tests of Grosse-Kunstleve et al., 2004, with the same relative tolerance) on the 672 right-handed ordered bases of those vectors. It returns a `NiggliFromSelling` with the cell, a right-handed basis in the input basis, the operator from the reduced basis (entries in {-1, 0, 1}) and the Niggli type, and agrees with `reduction.niggli_gk` on every cell tested. No HTTP or MCP change.
- `agentsg.cell.strain_closure`: `reindex` computes its candidates by the reindexing workflow of the manuscript's supporting information (Section S10): the reference's members are tabulated once as the 24 metrics each predicts in the new cell's reduced basis, where both input bases drop out of the price; one scale serves every candidate; a necessary condition on the ten dot products of the new reduced superbase (Lemma A7(i) with Proposition A4) discards orderings before any eigenvalue is computed; and the exact operator is built only within the band. The candidates are those of the direct form, with prices equal to rounding, about twenty times faster on the benchmark. `reindex_workflow(reference, new_cell, ...)` returns a `ReindexWorkflow` with every intermediate quantity (member and ordering counts, the common scale, the bounds, the priced orderings, the candidates, the time per step). No HTTP or MCP change.
- `agentsg.cell.strain_closure`: docstrings follow the manuscript's renumbered appendix (Lemma A1 the class excess identity, A2 the window, A7 widening one side, Remark A8 the layers and the lookup). No code, HTTP or MCP change.
- `agentsg.cell.strain_closure`: `new_sg_hm=None` means primitive in both `reindex` and `indexing_classes`, and both default to the reference's centring. An `R` symbol without `:H`/`:R` is read on hexagonal axes for a hexagonal-shaped cell (within 5°), on rhombohedral axes with a warning for a rhombohedral-shaped one, and otherwise raises. `reindex` warns when no candidate is within the band (a C cell read as I has the same volume). No HTTP or MCP change.
- `agentsg.cell.strain_closure`: `reindex` reads the new cell with the reference's centring by default, as `indexing_classes` does (`new_sg_hm=None` for primitive); the volume check matches the edge-scale limit in both directions ((1 − m)³ to (1 + m)³); candidates of equal price are listed nearest the identity first; an `R` symbol without `:H`/`:R` on a cell that is not hexagonal-shaped is read on rhombohedral axes, with a warning; `indexing_classes` warns when the reference lacks the lattice symmetry of the given space group within the band. No HTTP or MCP change.
- `agentsg.cell.strain_closure`: `R ... :R` symbols are read as primitive (the rhombohedral axes), not with the hexagonal centring; an unreadable space-group symbol raises `ValueError` instead of being read as P; malformed cells (not six numbers, non-positive edges, angles outside (0°, 180°), no positive-definite metric) raise `ValueError`, and angles that all look like radians warn. `indexing_classes` reads the new cell with the reference's centring unless `new_sg_hm` is given, validates the space group before any reindexing, and caches the reference's self-maps on the widened closure. `reindex` refuses a volume ratio beyond what `max_scale_change` allows ((1 + m)³), so no candidate is dropped silently by the scale limit. The exact enumeration raises `ValueError` when the ball of Lemma A6 exceeds `MAX_BALL` = 5000 lattice vectors (thin needles at large bands) instead of running out of memory; `widened_closure(..., max_ball=None)` lifts it. The default price band is `DEFAULT_ACCEPT` = 7.4 %, with the reference widened at `band_for(DEFAULT_ACCEPT)`. `universal_operators()` returns the 6096 elements of U as column operators W for a lookup P = B_R W B_N⁻¹ (the stored data are superbase rows, the transpose). The deprecated `eps` alias of `reindex` and `indexing_classes` now warns. No HTTP or MCP change.
- `agentsg.cell.strain_closure`: candidate prices are the affine-invariant (geodesic) distance between the new metric and the predicted one after the best common scale, a true distance on cell shapes, so price bounds on products and cosets are exact. `band_for(accept)` is now `exp(accept) - 1` and `accept_for(eps)` its inverse; `LEPAGE_PRICE` is 2√2 artanh(sin 3°). `reindex` rejects a fitted scale that changes edge lengths by more than `max_scale_change` (default 10 %; `None` disables) and raises `ValueError` when the two primitive cells differ in volume by a factor of 1.6 or more (wrong centring symbol, sub- or superlattice). `IndexingResult.proper_group_order` gives the order of the proper rotations of the Laue (or point) group. `sort_spectrum` uses an exact constrained projection. `band_for`, `accept_for`, `LEPAGE_PRICE`, `price_spectrum`, `strain_from_esds` and `certified_sort_spectrum` are exported. No HTTP or MCP change.
- `agentsg.cell.strain_closure`: `reindex`, `indexing_classes` and `price_spectrum` return proper operators (positive determinant; +1, or +1/k in a centred reference's conventional basis) by default (`proper=True`); a left-handed basis is never a valid reindexing. Pass `proper=None` for the old behaviour. `strain_from_esds(cell, esds)` gives the root-mean-square strain of a cell from its standard uncertainties, for setting the band (about 2.5 times it, plus an allowance for non-isomorphism). `certified_sort_spectrum(cell)` returns the sort spectrum with the adjacency radius δ₁ below which its values are exact; a larger value is an upper bound over the frames used. No HTTP or MCP change.
- `agentsg.cell.strain_closure`: `reindex` prices candidates at the new cell after a common scale fitted in the strain norm (not the Frobenius norm), takes `accept` (the price band) with the reference widened at `band_for(accept)` = accept/(1−accept), and `proper=True` to drop improper candidates; `price_spectrum(ref)` lists the prices of the reference's self-maps; `indexing_classes` takes `anomalous=True` to group by the point group instead of the Laue group, and `proper`; `LEPAGE_PRICE` = 2 × `LEPAGE_STRAIN`. `tier` is renamed `layer` (`tier`, `laue_order`, `laue_in_symmetry` and the `eps` argument of `reindex`/`indexing_classes` stay as deprecated aliases for one release). No HTTP or MCP change.
- `agentsg.cell.strain_closure` computes the widened Selling closure of a measured cell in the strain norm ‖G^-1/2 ΔG G^-1/2‖_F, which is the same in every basis. `widened_closure(cell, sg_hm, eps)` enumerates it exactly (the finite ball of Lemma A6) and reports the tier that holds it: 1 for the 127 superbases of the first adjacency layer, 2 for the 2755 of the second. The default band is Le Page-style, one cell angle changed by 3°, a strain of 0.074. `reindex(reference, new_cell)` returns every candidate operator within the band, exact and ranked by strain price. `sort_spectrum(cell)` gives the strain distance to each of Delone's 24 sorts. `indexing_classes(reference, new_cell, space_group)` groups the candidates into the classes of the reference's Laue group inside the band's (pseudo-)symmetry group, one priced representative per physically distinct indexing (4 for P3, 2 for P3₁21 and P2₁3, 2 for a pseudo-tetragonal P2₁2₁2₁ cell, 1 for P4₃2₁2). Needs numpy and scipy. No HTTP or MCP change.

## 0.3.8

- `pdb_app build-cod` writes a second cell database that holds only Crystallography Open Database entries. That file has no UniProt or deposition-group columns. `add-cod` still inserts those rows into an existing database.
- `python -m agentsg.cell.cod_explorer` opens that archive as three principal-component views of the volume-normalised sorted linear key, with a rotatable 3D panel. Each key is divided by the cube root of the primitive-cell volume. A click shows the deposited cell, the Selling cell, and the chemical formula. The explorer does not read UniProt or deposition-group columns.

## 0.3.7

- The cell database takes Crystallography Open Database entries next to the PDB ones: `CellDatabase.add_cod(path)` reads a COD table export (CSV/TSV) or a tree of COD CIFs and stores each cell as `COD<id>` with `source = 'cod'`, its esds (`sig_a` … `sig_gamma`) and chemical formula; rhombohedral-axes cells (`:R`) are treated as primitive. Existing rows are marked `source = 'pdb'`.
- `pdb_search` hits and `pdb_lookup` report `source` (and `formula` when there is one).

## 0.3.6

## 0.3.1

- Short monoclinic names (`P21/n`, `C2/n`, …), parenthesised screws (`P2(1)/c`), and ITA `number:setting` codes (`14:b2`, `14:c1`, `68:1`) resolve to the tabulated setting. An ambiguous short name assumes unique axis b and reports `assumed`.
- `ita_plate` accepts a bare or parenthesised change of basis together with `sg`, a full `symbol (cob)` setting, the same string in `sg`, and `R` on an R group.
- A symbol in `setting` must be the same IT number as `sg`. `setting="P"` against `C 2 2 21` is an error. A bare lattice letter is not a change of basis.
- `setting` reports `centring_from`, `centring_to`, and `centring_changed` from the translation coset. `added_centering` remains for one release, true only when the new cell has more centring vectors, and is marked deprecated.
- Orthorhombic `projection="all"` panel titles are the ITA symbol of the panel's change of basis, with that cob in the subtitle. An along-b view of C222₁ is A2₁22, not C22₁2.
- Concept-graph anchors were refreshed from this commit.
