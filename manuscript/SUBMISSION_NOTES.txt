IUCr submission source
========================

Journal: Journal of Applied Crystallography.
The working files are manuscript/submission/zwart_selling_closure.tex
and zwart_selling_closure_SI.tex, prepared with iucr.cls and
\journalcode{J}. In this class file J is J. Appl. Cryst. (ISSN
0021-8898). IUCrJ is \journalcode{M}, and Acta Cryst. A is
\journalcode{A}. An earlier note in this file treated J as IUCrJ;
that was wrong. main_v9.tex and "main _v8.tex" stay as history.

Build, from manuscript/submission/:
    pdflatex zwart_selling_closure.tex
    pdflatex zwart_selling_closure_SI.tex

What a rerun of manuscript/appendix_z put back into the text
-----------------------------------------------------------
Only numbers the current code reproduced are printed. Holds:

- Equal-step raw reduced distances are 0.080 and 118.5; sorted-key
  distance to the reference is 0.0010 on both sides. The old raw
  figure 83.7 is not used.
- KD-tree on 206214 precomputed keys: build 0.044 s, median k=10
  query 0.010 ms (Python 3.12, NumPy 2.5.3, SciPy 1.18.1). The old
  0.28 s and 0.04 ms are not this measurement.
- Entropy effective rank, k=40 on a 30000-cell subsample: 1.00 to
  5.69, median 2.02. The old median 4.12 is not reproduced.
- Reindexing wall clock on this machine: 6960-matrix median 15 ms
  per frame, cached procedure median 75 ms per frame, reference
  closure enumerated once. The old 0.07 ms and 330-fold figures
  are not used.
- XFEL orientation (CXIDB 83 stream): c MAD 0.22 A for beam within
  10 degrees of c* (n=141) versus 0.083 A above 80 degrees
  (n=2866), ratio 2.6. The old 1.53 A and 0.5 A are not this binning.
  c median 233.3 A, MAD 0.13 A, maximum 245 A.
- Niggli change-of-basis identity holds on a 500-cell noisy sample
  (0 failures, max error 8e-13). The old report of identity failure
  and 1-2 percent non-convergence is not reproduced.
- Pseudo-symmetry checks are the two hand-imposed strata only.
- The edge-fidelity fraction is not reported. Do not read the
  embedding as topology.
