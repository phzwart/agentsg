MX Agents

Crystallography tools for people and agents.

Space-group and symmetry agents

These answer a space-group question from the operators. They look up a group and its setting, reflection conditions, sites, subgroups, and ITA plates, and they compare a unit cell with cells deposited in the PDB. No access token.

  • sg-muse — HTTP API. Ask it directly, or point an HTTP connector at it.
  • sg-mcp — the same tools over MCP, for an agent client.

Unit-cell embedding

A map of deposited unit cells. Explore the shape of cell space, select a region, and colour the selection by UniProt accession or deposition group.

  • cell-embed — the explorer.
