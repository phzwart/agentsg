What holds across all settings: one component carries 80–100 % of anchors, and with the two-dimensional filter it is a mesh with 27–92 independent loops, not a tree with arms. Branch nodes are everywhere (225 of 255 anchors sit in one in at least one graph), the two filters disagree on where they are (Spearman 0.06), and branch frequency does not follow how many sequence segments a window touches (p = 0.83). It leans weakly toward central, less mobile anchors (centrality +0.41, mobility −0.29). The 130s unit (123–140) separates as its own component in 8 of 36 graphs; elsewhere it stays joined through its packing neighbours. The one-dimensional eccentricity filter gives trees by construction, so its loop count is zero by design.
